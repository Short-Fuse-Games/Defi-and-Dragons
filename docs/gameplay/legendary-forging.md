# 🔥 Legendary Equipment Forging Guide

Welcome, master craftsman! The art of forging legendary equipment is a complex but rewarding process that combines your combat prowess, resource management, and DeFi engagement.

## Overview

Legendary Forging allows players to create powerful, unique equipment by combining:
- Base equipment pieces
- LP tokens from specific pools
- Combat achievements
- Time investment

## The Forging Process

### 1. Prerequisites
- Required base equipment
- Sufficient LP tokens
- Minimum combat score
- Required special materials (if any)

### 2. Steps
1. **Start Forging**: Initiate the forging process for your chosen recipe
2. **Lock Materials**: Commit your base equipment and LP tokens
3. **Wait**: Allow the forging time to complete
4. **Complete**: Claim your legendary equipment

## Benefits of Legendary Equipment

1. **Enhanced Stats**: Significantly higher stat bonuses than regular equipment
2. **Unique Effects**: Special abilities and combat mechanics
3. **Prestige**: Rare and valuable within the game economy
4. **Trading Potential**: Can be traded on the marketplace

## Tips for Forging

1. **Plan Ahead**: Ensure you have all materials before starting
2. **Time Management**: Consider the forging time when planning
3. **Resource Efficiency**: Compare recipes to find the best value
4. **Combat Score**: Build up your combat score before attempting difficult recipes

## Technical Details

### Combat Score Calculation
Combat score is determined by:
- Monsters defeated
- Boss battles won
- Quest completions
- PvP victories

### LP Token Requirements
- Must be from valid pools
- Tokens are locked during forging
- Cannot withdraw once committed

### Forging Time
- Cannot be shortened
- Must wait full duration
- Time starts after materials are locked

## Common Issues

### Troubleshooting
1. **Cannot Start Forging**
   - Check combat score requirement
   - Verify recipe is active
   - Ensure you have all materials

2. **Cannot Lock Materials**
   - Check LP token balances
   - Verify equipment ownership
   - Confirm approvals are set

3. **Cannot Complete**
   - Wait for forging time
   - Ensure materials are locked
   - Verify process wasn't interrupted
